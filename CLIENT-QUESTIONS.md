## 5. Workflow & Standard Overhaul Protocol Confirmation

The website publishes a standard **7-Stage Engineering Protocol** for motor and compressor overhauls:
1. **Receive & Incoming Inspection** (visual inspection, electrical insulation resistance, runout checks)
2. **Dismantle & Diagnostic Testing** (surge comparison, core loop loss testing, micrometer bearing fit)
3. **Rewinding & Precision Machining** (Class H/F copper rewinding, shaft metallizing, housing sleeving)
4. **Varnish & Thermal Curing** (dip/VPI impregnating, thermostatically controlled oven baking)
5. **Dynamic Balancing & Reassembly** (dual-plane balancing to ISO 1940 G2.5, new SKF/FAG bearings)
6. **Final Quality Assurance & Run Test** (no-load run test, vibration spectrum, full QA test certificate)
7. **Packaging & Riyadh On-Site Delivery** (moisture barrier wrapping, crating, transport)
