FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is a three phase electric motor and where is it used?

A three phase electric motor runs on a three-phase AC power supply and is commonly used in industrial applications like cooling towers, pumps, compressors, conveyors, and heavy machinery due to its high efficiency, smooth operation, and ability to handle heavier loads compared to single phase motors.

Q2. What is the difference between single phase and three phase motors?

Single phase motors are typically used for smaller loads (up to 7.5 HP) in homes or light commercial use, while three phase motors are built for industrial-scale loads, offering higher starting torque, better efficiency, and more stable power output — making them the standard choice for factories and industrial units.

Q3. What HP/KW range of three phase motors does Rawat Industries manufacture?

We manufacture Cromtech series three phase motors from 0.18 KW to 22 KW, in frame sizes 63 to 180, available in 2, 4, 6, and 8 pole speed configurations to suit different industrial applications.

Q4. Do you provide customized Electric Motors for specific applications?

Yes, we manufacture customized electric motors based on customer requirements — including specific voltage, frequency, mounting type, and application needs such as cooling towers, exhaust systems, or specialized industrial machinery.

Q5. How do I know if my Electric Motor needs rewinding or repair?

Common signs include unusual noise or vibration, overheating, reduced efficiency, burning smell, or the motor tripping frequently. Rawat Industries offers free diagnostics to accurately identify the issue before recommending rewinding or repair.

Q6. What is the typical lifespan of a three phase industrial motor?

With proper maintenance, a good quality three phase motor typically lasts 10-15 years or more. Regular servicing, correct load usage, and timely rewinding when needed can significantly extend motor life.

Q7. Do you offer motor rewinding services for Electric motors?

Yes, Rawat Industries provides complete rewinding and reconditioning services for Electric motors of all sizes, using quality materials to restore original performance and efficiency.

Q8. What industries commonly use Rawat Industries' three phase motors?

Our motors are widely used in cooling tower systems, HVAC applications, pumping stations, manufacturing units, and general industrial machinery across Delhi NCR and other parts of India.

Q9. Do you deliver/supply Electric Motors outside Delhi?

Yes, we supply industrial Electric motors across India, in addition to serving Delhi NCR directly from our Bawana Industrial Area facility.
