# Tarfa Equipment Maintenance — Photography & Visual Asset Log

This document serves as the photographic production brief for Tarfa Equipment Maintenance. It catalogues every visual slot on the website, its current status (extracted PDF image or temporary placeholder), required technical specifications, and shot-by-shot artistic direction.

---

## 1. Photography Style & Art Direction

To preserve Tarfa's 40-year reputation as an authentic, industrial-grade engineering firm, all replacement imagery must adhere to the following documentary guidelines:

- **Aesthetic:** High-contrast, documentary-style industrial photography. Emphasize heavy iron, copper wire coils, machine lathe shavings, steel laminations, and genuine workshop wear (patina, clean machine oil, crane chains).
- **Lighting:** Natural workshop ambient lighting (overhead bay fluorescents, tungsten work lamps) combined with subtle rim lighting. Avoid clinical, sterile stock-photography lighting or saturated corporate blue hues.
- **Composition:** Strong directional lines, shallow depth of field focusing on technical hand-craftsmanship (e.g. inserting insulation slot liners, winding copper coils, brazing commutator bars, aligning dial indicators on balancing machine).
- **People & Attire:** Technicians in real industrial PPE (safety glasses, steel-toe boots, heavy-duty workshop overalls/workwear). No stiff posed models, corporate suits, or clean clipboards.
- **Strictly Prohibited:** Glossy 3D renders, AI-generated images with phantom reflections/distorted fingers, stock photos of modern semiconductor cleanrooms, or generic white-background clip art.

## 3. Submission Guidelines for the Client

1. **Format:** Submit original high-resolution uncompressed camera files (`RAW`, `.CR3`, `.NEF`, or uncompressed `TIFF`/`PNG`).
2. **Resolution:** Minimum horizontal resolution of **2400 px** at 72 DPI (preferably 300 DPI for archival).
3. **Delivery:** Upload to shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder structured by `workshop/`, `services/`, and `facility/`.
4. **Processing:** The front-end engineering team will perform uniform color calibration, desaturation, sharpening, and WebP/AVIF compression according to the site's art direction rules.
